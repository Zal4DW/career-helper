## Status Definitions

- Applied: submitted, awaiting response
- Phone Screen: recruiter or hiring manager intro call booked or completed
- Interview: formal interview round in progress
- Final Round: last stage before decision
- Offer: verbal or written offer received
- Rejected: formal rejection received
- Withdrawn: removed self from process
- No Response: no reply after 3+ weeks
