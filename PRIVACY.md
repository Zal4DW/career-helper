# Privacy Statement

**Plugin:** Career Helper
**Publisher:** Prosper AI Consulting, UK

Career Helper is an open source plugin that runs entirely inside your Claude account. Prosper AI Consulting receives no data, runs no servers, and collects no analytics or telemetry. We do not know who has installed the plugin.

Everything the plugin produces is saved in your own workspace or in artifacts, Claude Docs, and slide decks in your own Claude account, and belongs to you. Artifacts are private to you unless you choose to share them. Your use of Claude, including any web research or browsing the plugin asks Claude to do on your behalf, is covered by Anthropic's terms and privacy policy.

## What the plugin does

- **Reads and writes files** in the workspace folder you give it (CVs, trackers, plans, and reports).
- **Publishes artifacts, Claude Docs, and slide decks** in your Claude account when a skill offers one and you agree.
- **Runs web research** through Claude's own tools when a skill needs public information, such as company research or job listings.
- **Uses a signed-in browser** (Cowork's built-in browser or Claude in Chrome) only when you ask it to search a site you are signed in to.
- **Installs two Python packages** (`markdown` and `weasyprint`) in your Cowork environment when you ask for a verified CV PDF.

The plugin sends nothing to Prosper AI Consulting or to any service of its own.
